Pipeline Rush: the recruiter game

You have 60 seconds to hire the right candidates. How high can you score?

October 2026

A 60-second game for recruiters: move candidates from first contact to hired before they ghost you, and reject the red flags.

How to play

  • Press Advance to move a candidate to the next stage: To contact → Contacted → Interview → Hired.
  • Each candidate has a timer bar: if it runs out they ghost you and you lose a life.
  • Candidates with a red flag (red card) should be rejected. Hiring them costs a life.
  • You have 3 lives and 60 seconds. The longer you play, the faster it gets.

Press Start: you have 60 seconds and 3 lives.

In real life there is no timer
