Candidates

Full candidate record

Name, phone, email, city, role and availability (on-site, hybrid, remote). Plus pipeline stage, source, client company if you work for third parties, star rating and colored tags.

Pipeline and Kanban view

You decide the stages: add, rename, reorder or delete them. See candidates as a list or in Kanban and drag them from one stage to another.

Resumes that fill themselves in

Upload a PDF or Word resume and the program recognizes name, phone, email, city, role and skills, and fills the empty fields. Fields already filled are never overwritten. Always check the data before saving.

Swap first/last name, copy contact data

If the name comes out inverted from the resume, one click swaps it. “Copy contact data” puts name, city, phone and email on the clipboard, ready to paste.

Second, anonymous resume

Attach a second version of the resume without personal data, to use when you present the candidate to the client.

Notes, history and privacy consent

Free notes and a dated history for every call or update. You record the GDPR consent and the program reminds you when it is missing.

Export and import a candidate

“Export candidate” saves the whole record, resumes included, in a file. A colleague using the same program loads it with “Import candidate”.

Excel and trash

Import and export in Excel, even just the candidates you choose. Deleted candidates stay recoverable for 30 days.

Find and contact

Find candidates from a job description

Paste the position (or upload a PDF or Word file) and the program extracts role, skills and years of experience. Then it prepares searches on Google and Bing, by city and skill combinations. You run them in your own browser.

Reading the results

Copy the results page (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C) and press “Paste and read”: cards appear with name, role, company, city and the skills found, with a score. New profiles are added without duplicates.

Country and message language

Search 21 countries or worldwide. The program switches the LinkedIn subdomain by itself. Messages are in Italian for Italy and in English for other countries, but you can change the language at any time.

Connection note and message

For each profile the program prepares the note to attach to the connection request (within LinkedIn’s 300 characters) and the message after acceptance. You copy and send them yourself: the program sends nothing on its own.

WhatsApp

An icon next to each candidate opens the chat with the message ready, in the browser or in the app. Numbers with + or 00 are used as they are, otherwise your default prefix is used. “Mark as sent” writes a note in the history.

Message templates and interview invite

Templates for LinkedIn and email filled with your details. For the interview enter date, time and link (Teams, Zoom, Meet): the link stays clickable in Outlook and Gmail.

Ready-to-post LinkedIn announcement

Paste a client request (or upload the PDF or Word file) and get an announcement to paste on LinkedIn, with title, activities, requirements and your contact. The client code and the rate stay out.

Search everywhere (Ctrl+K)

From any page, search candidates, sheets and templates. One click on the result opens the right record.

Job match

Match for one position

Paste the job description and the program extracts the requirements, which you can remove, add or make mandatory. You get a ranking with a percentage, found requirements and missing ones.

Several positions from an Excel file

Upload a sheet with one row per position and compare candidates against all of them at once. For each position you see the top 10; for each candidate you see the positions they may fit. The result can be saved to Excel.

Everything on your PC

The match uses no online artificial intelligence and does not change candidate data. It reads the resumes once and then remembers them, so later comparisons are fast.

How to read the score

The percentage is an aid to decide where to start, not a judgment. A skill mentioned several times weighs more than one mentioned once. Required years of experience are shown but should be checked by hand.

Organize

Dashboard

Total candidates, overdue or due-today callbacks, hires and conversion rate. A notice flags anyone you have not contacted for over 14 days.

Callbacks with reminders

The list of calls to make in due-date order, with a Windows notification when the time comes.

Hires and charts

A history of positions closed successfully, and charts by stage, by month and by source generated from your data.

Excel-style sheets

Create sheets with text, number, date or pick-from-list columns with validation. Conditional colors, resizable rows and columns, and copy/paste with Excel in both directions.

Many sheets, no clutter

Recent-sheets bar, full list with search, deletion of several sheets at once with an automatic safety copy before deleting.

Data and security

Your data stays on your PC

Candidates, sheets and settings live in a folder separate from the program. Updating or uninstalling never touches it. The internet is only needed to activate the license.

Automatic backups

Automatic safety copies (at most one every 6 hours, keeping the last 30), always available from the “Automatic backups” button. You can also save or restore a backup by hand.

Safe saving

If the PC shuts down suddenly, no file is left half-written. If a file gets damaged, the program restores the latest valid backup.

Automatic updates

When a new version is out the program tells you and offers to install it, without touching the data you have already entered.

Trial and license

Free 14-day trial from the first launch. Then the program asks for the license key, valid for one PC. The trial can be downloaded and used on other computers too.

Italian and English

Change language at any time, with no restart: menus, records, messages, default stages and sources are translated.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I use it on more than one PC?

Each license is for a single PC. The 14-day free trial, however, can be downloaded and used on other computers too, so you can check it works where you need it before buying.

How long is the free trial?

14 days from first launch, then the program asks for a license to continue.

Windows or my browser shows a warning when I download. Is it safe?

Yes. The message (“isn't commonly downloaded” in Edge, “Windows protected your PC” when opening) appears for new or little-used programs and does not mean there is a problem with the file. In your browser choose Keep (in Edge: the three dots next to the file, then Keep and Keep anyway). When the blue Windows screen appears, click More info and then Run anyway. The program runs on your computer and your data stays local.

Is there a subscription?

No. The full license costs a one-time €79 and is lifetime.

Do I need an internet connection?

JobDesk CRM is designed to work offline and your data stays on your PC. An internet connection is only needed to activate the license the first time.

Which operating system does it run on?

The current version is built for Windows.

Is it available in other languages?

Yes, JobDesk CRM is available in Italian and English. You can switch languages at any time from the program's menu, with no reinstalling needed.

What happens to my data when I update?

Updates never touch the separate folder where your data and settings are stored.

How do I receive my license?

After purchase you'll receive the license key by email: paste it into the program on first launch to activate it.

Can I get a refund after purchasing?

Before buying, you get 14 days of free trial to check that the software fits your needs, with no commitment. Because of this, once the purchase is completed and the digital license has been delivered, refunds are not available: the purchase is considered final.
