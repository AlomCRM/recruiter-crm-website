FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long is the free trial?

14 days from first launch, then the program asks for a license to continue.

Is there a subscription?

No. The full license costs a one-time €79 and is lifetime.

Do I need an internet connection?

Recruiter CRM is designed to work offline and your data stays on your PC. An internet connection is only needed to activate the license the first time.

Which operating system does it run on?

The current version is built for Windows.

Is it available in other languages?

Yes, Recruiter CRM is available in Italian and English. You can switch languages at any time from the program's menu, with no reinstalling needed.

What happens to my data when I update?

Updates never touch the separate folder where your data and settings are stored.

How do I receive my license?

After purchase you'll receive the license key by email: paste it into the program on first launch to activate it.
